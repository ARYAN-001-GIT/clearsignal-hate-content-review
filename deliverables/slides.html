The challenge

Short posts carry context.

  • Irony and quotation can change a sentence's meaning.
  • Local language and reclaimed terms are easy to misread.
  • A label alone gives a reviewer little to inspect.
Research questionCan a lightweight text model show useful evidence and learn from human corrections while keeping a held-out evaluation?

Responsible use

What these baselines cannot establish

  • Language and slang hints can be wrong.
  • HASOC is limited to its source labels and languages.
  • OCR and text models do not interpret image meaning.
  • No conversation history, LLM or learned reward model.
Human review stays centralNever use a prototype score alone to punish a person or remove content.

This app is a classroom research tool, not a moderation system.
